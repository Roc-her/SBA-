A buyer's agent should make the decision clearer, not louder.

The role is advocacy, structure and protection.

## The Brief Comes First

Good support starts by making the brief specific.

That means budget, lifestyle fit, location logic and the risks you are not willing to carry.

## The Property Gets Tested

The next job is to test the property against data, contract terms and emotion.

Sometimes the best advice is to stop.

## The Decision Stays Yours

You still make the decision.

Sarah helps you make it with more calm, evidence and protection.
