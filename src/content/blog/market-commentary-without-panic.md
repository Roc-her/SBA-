Market commentary can make every week feel like a turning point.

Most buyers do not need another prediction. They need a decision framework.

## Headlines Are Not Strategy

A headline is written for attention.

Your strategy should be built around borrowing capacity, timing, suburb fit and risk tolerance.

## Local Detail Matters

Regional Victoria does not move as one single market.

Street quality, buyer demand, stock levels and property condition can matter more than the broad national story.

## Stay Close To The Decision

The useful question is not always, "What is the market doing?"

Often it is, "Does this property make sense for this buyer, at this price, with this risk?"
